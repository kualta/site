KAOMOJI SOURCES
Collected 2026-09-29

The existing themed library is retained. Imported expressions are trimmed,
normalized to Unicode NFC, and deduplicated; their category, tag and reading
labels are unioned across sources. Internal spacing and glyph widths are retained.
Standalone prose, emoji and decorations were filtered from imported collections.
Source membership and labels are retained in src/data/kaomoji-collected.json,
with source IDs in src/data/kaomoji-sources.json in https://github.com/kualta/site.
